# TechStore Frequently Asked Questions

## Shipping
We offer free standard shipping on all orders over 200 dinars. Standard shipping takes 2 to 4 working days. Express shipping (next day) is available for 15 dinars.

## Returns and Refunds
You can return any product within 14 days of purchase, as long as it is in its original box with the receipt. Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 working days. Opened software and earphones cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

## Warranty
All new products come with a 12-month manufacturer warranty. Refurbished items carry a 6-month TechStore warranty. You can buy an extended warranty within 30 days of purchase.

## Payment
We accept cash, bank cards, and payment in 3 installments for purchases above 900 dinars.
